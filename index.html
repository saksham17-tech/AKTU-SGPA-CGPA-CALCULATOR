How It Works

SGPA Formula

SGPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) / Σ(Credits)

Each subject's internal and external marks are converted to a percentage of that subject's total marks (100 for both Theory and Practical subjects). That percentage maps to a grade point using the official scale below, which is then weighted by credits.

YGPA Formula

YGPA = (SGPA₁ × Credits₁ + SGPA₂ × Credits₂) / (Credits₁ + Credits₂)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) measures your academic performance for a single semester.

What is YGPA?

YGPA (Yearly Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of both semesters in an academic year.

Does this calculator save my data?

Yes. Each semester's subjects are auto-saved separately in your browser, so switching semesters never overwrites another semester's data, and your entries are still there even after closing the browser or returning much later.
